# Retrieval quality + speed

Queries: **20** from `bench/queries/kb-docs.jsonl`.

Speed is **warm-cache** end-to-end wall-time per query (the whole query set is fired once untimed first, so the query-embed cache is warm — these numbers isolate the search *pipeline* from the one-time per-query embed); `srv p50` is the daemon's own `ms` (search compute only). Quality higher = better; latency lower = better.

## Mode: `hybrid`

### Quality

| kb | Recall@1 | Recall@5 | Recall@10 | MRR | nDCG@10 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| kb-docs-small | 0.650 | 0.950 | 0.950 | 0.771 | 0.816 |
| kb-docs-base | 0.750 | 0.950 | 0.950 | 0.825 | 0.856 |
| kb-docs-large | 0.800 | 0.950 | 0.950 | 0.863 | 0.885 |

### Speed (ms; lower = faster)

| kb | p50 | p95 | mean | srv p50 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| kb-docs-base | 9.0 | 10.4 | 9.1 | 8.0 |
| kb-docs-large | 8.8 | 11.4 | 9.3 | 8.0 |
