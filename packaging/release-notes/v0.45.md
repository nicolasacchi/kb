## kb v0.45

### N3

- CI: new `first-run` workflow installs the published release (tarball via the
  tag's `install.sh`, with checksum and provenance verification) on clean
  debian, ubuntu, fedora and arm64 containers, pulls the ghcr image anonymously,
  and checks the SPA, keyword search, the watcher and `kb daemon doctor`. It runs
  after each release and on manual dispatch, and reports without gating.
