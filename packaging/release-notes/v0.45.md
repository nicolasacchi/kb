### N4

- Fixed: the codex, opencode, kimi, omp and grok capture adapters now spool the session on capture failure instead of losing it (the v0.44 Known issue "adapters do not spool"); a spooled item is replayed, scrubbed, by the next successful capture.
- Fixed: non-UUID session ids no longer share a capture file (`a_b` and `a-b` used to collide); `kb sessions capture --stamp` names a new capture after the session's real start time. Existing lossy-named captures keep working and are never renamed.
