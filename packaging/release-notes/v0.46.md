## kb v0.46 — omp capture repair, segmented-capture read side

v0.46 fixes omp session capture wasting CPU on large sessions, makes spool
replays safe for every harness, and ships the read side of segmented capture
(dark: nothing produces segments yet).

### Before you upgrade

- **Schema migration V0045 (kb).** Two additive, nullable columns on
  `sessions` (`segment_of`, `segment_idx`) and a partial index. The migration
  advances the schema epoch, so an older `kb` refuses to open the upgraded
  state (`refuse_if_volume_ahead`). Run `kb backup --all` before upgrading;
  rollback means restoring that backup (`docs/self-host.md`). kb-code is
  unchanged (epoch 45).
- **Update the `kb` binary with, or before, the kb-memory hooks.** The rule
  that a spool replay never overwrites a fresher capture lives in the binary.
- **omp:** the new capture script applies to a running omp session at its
  next capture once the checkout or plugin the extension loads from is
  updated; the `kb-omp.ts` changes (owned, detached capture) apply on a fresh
  extension load. No session needs a restart.

### Fixes

- **omp capture no longer wastes CPU.** One conversion per session at a time
  across processes (a per-session lock over convert + land); newer requests
  are coalesced, never dropped, and a stale conversion can never publish after
  a fresher one. An unchanged session is not converted again (the fingerprint
  covers in-place title rewrites and sidecar changes) and is recorded as
  captured only after it actually landed. Every capture runs in its own
  session/process group: a timeout, cancellation, shutdown or SIGKILL reaps
  every descendant (`jq`, the `kb` writer) and never signals omp's own group;
  locks and scratch are released on every exit path and the previous capture
  stays intact. The leaf-chain translator is linear instead of quadratic
  (a 40k-line synthetic session: 181 s -> about 8 s, byte-identical output),
  and malformed tool-call records no longer lose the capture.
- **Turn-end captures are no longer cancelled at omp's 30 s stop budget**: the
  capture keeps running detached, one per session, bounded by its own
  deadline (`KB_CAPTURE_HARD_SECS`, default 120 s, plus the landing budget).
- **Spool replay never overwrites a fresher capture** (all adapters): a
  replayed item is dropped when the session's capture was published after the
  item was parked; the check and the atomic rename run under a per-session
  publish lock.
- **Docker image** also bakes the registry-default `bge-small-en-v1.5`
  (about 130 MB more, ~1.8 GB total), so a `kb.toml` without
  `embedding_model` no longer downloads a model at first boot and works
  offline.
- **Release smoke (`first-run`)**: the harness now runs from the default
  branch (only `install.sh` and the sample corpus come from the tag), counts
  hits from the real `kb search --json` shape, and no longer trips on the
  `[server]` block `kb add` writes or on SIGPIPE under `pipefail`.

### New (dark)

- **Segmented capture, read side.** A harness session whose translation
  outgrows a part target can be stored as an ordered chain of ordinary
  sessions (part 1 under the session id, parts 2+ under `<id>-pNN`).
  Every session-counting reader counts a chain once while event totals sum;
  `GET /api/sessions` and `kb sessions list` show every part with a
  `part k/N` chip and an opt-in `?collapse=logical` / `--collapse`; session
  detail carries the chain and the view its prev/next; live status groups a
  chain into one row; `kb sessions recover <id> --chain` joins the parts.
  Segment keys in a transcript are validated against the session id.
- **`kb sessions segment-plan`**: a streaming planner that mirrors the omp
  translator's leaf-chain rules and cuts at user-message boundaries (16 MiB
  raw target), with an incremental checkpoint and per-part model attribution.
  Nothing calls it yet.

### Known issues

- omp sessions whose translation exceeds the 48 MiB capture cap are still not
  captured: each change of such a session is converted once (now linear and
  one at a time) and the writer refuses the result, with a warning on the
  hook's stderr and no spool entry. The segmenting omp adapter, behind
  `KB_CAPTURE_SEGMENTS=1`, comes in a later release.
- After a SIGKILL of a capture's owner, orphan cleanup is load-dependent
  (about 2.4 s measured idle); a request waits up to
  `KB_CAPTURE_ORPHAN_WAIT_SECS` (8 s) and otherwise catches up at the next
  trigger.
